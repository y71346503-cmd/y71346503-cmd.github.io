NEXUS

A personal command center for one person's tasks, mail, calendar and study.

NEXUS is a desktop application for Windows that runs on its owner's own computer. It brings together tasks, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Drive files, Google Classroom assignments and Google Contacts in one window, so the owner can plan the day in one place.

NEXUS is not a public service: there are no sign-ups and no servers. It is used only by its author with their own Google accounts.
